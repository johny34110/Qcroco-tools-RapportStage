LUSAC (EA 4253), standing for University Laboratory of Applied Sciences of Cherbourg, was founded in 1994 \citep{LUSAC}. It is a renowned and dynamic research center located in Cherbourg, France. It is a component of the University of Caen Normandy, dedicated to innovation and discoveries in the field of applied sciences.

This laboratory was established with the primary aim of promoting scientific and technological advancements through cutting-edge research and collaborative development.

The laboratory's operations revolve around two major axes: fundamental research and applied research. The former aims to expand our understanding of applied sciences, while the latter strives to harness this knowledge to address practical challenges and meet industry demands.

Since its inception, LUSAC has introduced several technological innovations and has participated in numerous research projects, reflecting its commitment to high-quality scientific experience and technological development.\\

The research unit is currently organized around three teams:
\begin{itemize}
	\item "Energy Efficiency and Heat Transfers" (EffiTherm),
	\item "Electric Energy Storage and Materials" (SEEM),
	\item "Flows and Environment" (EE).
\end{itemize}

It is within this third team that I completed my internship.

\subsection{The Cnam-INTECHMER}

The National Institute of Marine Science and Technology (INTECHMER), a prestigious institution affiliated with the National Conservatory of Arts and Crafts (CNAM), is located in Cherbourg, Normandy. Cnam-INTECHMER is renowned for its expertise in maritime sciences and techniques and is committed to providing high-quality higher education, combining theory and practice, in various fields related to the sea.\\

Cnam-INTECHMER offers three distinct diplomas:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Technical Executive Production and Valorization of Marine Resources (CT-PVRM): This training prepares students to be quickly operational in the fields of production and valorization of the ocean's biological resources. Application areas include fisheries, animal and plant aquaculture, aquariology, processing and valorization of seafood, as well as marine biotechnologies.
	
	\item Bachelor Oceanographer - Prospector (OP): This training aims to train technical executives in various applied oceanographic fields. Application areas include renewable marine energies, underwater works, research, exploration and exploitation of marine mineral resources, the development of new measurement techniques, and the management of oceanographic data.
	
	\item Technical Executive Marine Environmental Engineering (GEM): This multidisciplinary training prepares technical executives in the fields of control, surveillance, and protection of the marine environment. Application areas include water quality control, impact studies of human activities on the environment and marine ecosystems, coastal protection and development, preservation and restoration of marine ecosystems, and pollution control.
\end{enumerate}

In addition to its commitment to education, Cnam-INTECHMER, which is part of the Flow and Environment team of LUSAC, also conducts cutting-edge fundamental research. Its work aims to deepen our understanding of marine phenomena and to develop new technologies and methods for studying and managing the marine environment. The Institute also plays an active role in disseminating scientific and technical culture, sharing the results of its research with the scientific community and the broader public \citep{CNAM}.\\

Cnam-INTECHMER's research hub is organized around three key areas: sedimentology, biology, and hydrology. While these areas are distinct, they are interconnected and require an integrated approach to address complex issues related to the marine environment.

This interdisciplinary approach is a key element of Cnam-INTECHMER's research strategy. It not only helps solve complex problems but also stimulates innovation and promotes research excellence.
